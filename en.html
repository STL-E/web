About Us

The Student Tech Lab is a student laboratory and closely affiliated competition team belonging to the Savaria Institute of Technology at the ELTE Savaria University Centre.

Our main profile is to provide space, equipment, professional assistance, and a community for realizing student ideas, project tasks, and independent developments. In addition, we work on our own initiatives and participate in projects established through corporate collaborations.

Our goal is for students to gain practical experience beyond classroom knowledge and to test themselves in real engineering, development, or competition tasks.

Modular Blind Spot Monitoring System

The aim of the project is to increase traffic safety for road vehicles.

The system we developed monitors the vehicle's hard-to-see areas (blind spots) and helps the driver via a display to detect whether a pedestrian or another vehicle is present.

The project was created for the national KTI–ELTE traffic safety idea competition, where it achieved 3rd place among the best entries.

×

5-axis 3D Printer

A student development focusing on an alternative approach to 3D printing.

The system allows us to step out of the limitations of traditional three-axis movement ranges and experiment with new printing strategies.

As a result of the project, our fellow student achieved 1st place at the National Scientific Students' Associations Conference (OTDK).

Development is currently ongoing, and experiments related to the system are continuing.

What else do we do?

Alongside larger projects, many smaller developments are running in the lab. We mostly deal with 3D printing and electronics, often on an experimental basis.

Model airplanes, robotic arms, audio equipment, and custom developments have also been created in the lab.
